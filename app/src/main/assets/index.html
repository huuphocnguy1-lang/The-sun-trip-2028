The Sun Trip 2028

This shows how the app would look and behave during the ride. The values are simulated: speed comes from the slope, wind and power (rider 120 W + motor 200 W), and the battery charges only from the solar panels (600 W roof panel + extra panel when switched on).

Time speed

Motor: use − / + on the Ride screen (0–350 W, steps of 25 W). Speed and range change at once.

Navigation: turn arrows and spoken directions follow the route (example turns in this demo). Tap the speaker to mute.

Music: pick songs from your device with “Add songs”. Talk: hold the big button; replies are simulated in this demo.

On the real phone: position and speed come from GPS, battery and solar from the bike over Bluetooth.
